# Disclosure Boundary

XCP Research publishes evidence about a bounded mechanism while keeping the transformation mechanism private.

## Public

- the experimental method and source-to-target evidence flow;
- calibration, validation, and holdout roles;
- strict sanitized evidence contracts;
- opaque cryptographic identities;
- selected aggregate measurements and tolerance authority;
- bounded decisions and explicit non-claims;
- an offline integrity and consistency verifier.

## Public, sanitized

- source and target observations reduced to status, identity, and aggregate fields;
- the differential result reduced to one measured delta, its tolerance, classification, and decision;
- the architecture reduced to one intentionally opaque private block;
- historical lifecycle evidence reduced to aggregate pass counts.

## Private

Raw traces, executable semantic representations, source interpretation, transformation and target-generation logic, detailed capability mapping, diagnostic stage names, orchestration, and current research-frontier material are not published.

## Forbidden

Secrets, credentials, pairing data, local addresses, local paths, session identifiers, signing material, private endpoints, proprietary binaries, and material with uncertain redistribution rights must never enter this repository.

Default classification is private. Opaque hashes prove binding; they do not imply redistribution of the referenced private artifacts.
